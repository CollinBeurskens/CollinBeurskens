# Academic Resources

This page compiles a concise list of trusted academic and research resources, including platforms, tools, and educational sites, to support students and researchers. All links are clickable when viewed on platforms like GitHub.

## Research Platforms
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) - Search scholarly articles and papers.
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) - Biomedical and life sciences research database.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) - Open-access preprints in sciences.
- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/) - Academic journals, books, and primary sources.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) - Share and discover research papers.
- [Scopus](https://www.scopus.com/) - Citation database for peer-reviewed literature.
- [Web of Science](https://www.webofscience.com/) - Scientific citation indexing.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) - AI-driven research paper search.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) - Directory of Open Access Journals.
- [SpringerLink](https://link.springer.com/) - Scientific journals and books.
- [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/) - Technical literature in engineering and technology.
- [Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/) - Platform for sharing academic papers.
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) - Open-access biomedical research.
- [SSRN](https://www.ssrn.com/) - Preprints in social sciences and humanities.
- [SciFinder](https://scifinder.cas.org/) - Chemical and scientific literature search.

## Tools for Researchers
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) - Free citation management tool.
- [Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/) - Reference manager and academic network.
- [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) - Online LaTeX editor for papers.
- [ORCID](https://orcid.org/) - Unique identifiers for researchers.
- [EndNote](https://endnote.com/) - Citation and reference management software.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Writing assistant for academic work.
- [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/) - Free data visualization tool.
- [RefWorks](https://www.refworks.com/) - Web-based reference management.
- [LaTeX Project](https://www.latex-project.org/) - Typesetting system for academic documents.
- [Figshare](https://figshare.com/) - Share research data and outputs.

## Open Educational Resources
- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) - Free MIT course materials.
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) - Free online courses and tutorials.
- [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/) - Online courses from top universities.
- [edX](https://www.edx.org/) - Massive open online courses.
- [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/) - Free, peer-reviewed textbooks.
